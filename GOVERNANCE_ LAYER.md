1. Purpose of the Governance Layer

The Governance Layer is the first and highest authority in the planetary‑sensing‑system.
It defines the law, identity, evidence, and safety rules that every agent, world, and mesh node must obey.

It is the constitutional backbone of the entire digital civilization.

This layer ensures:

• Every actor is verified
• Every action is lawful
• Every movement is logged
• Every boundary is respected
• Every anomaly is contained


No agent, world, or subsystem can bypass governance.

---

2. Components of the Governance Layer

2.1 Identity Spine (QR‑DNA, Lineage Keys)

The Identity Spine verifies who is acting and what authority they carry.

It provides:

• QR‑DNA identity signatures
• Lineage keys (heritage‑based identity)
• Authority classification (CIV / CORP / MIL)
• Immutable identity binding across worlds
• Cross‑domain verification


Identity is the first gate.
Nothing proceeds without it.

---

2.2 Evidence Ledger (Immutable, Cross‑Domain)

The Evidence Ledger is the truth engine of the ecosystem.

It records:

• Every decision
• Every transaction
• Every anomaly
• Every cross‑world interaction
• Every agent action


All entries are:

• Immutable
• Cryptographically signed
• Cross‑domain accessible
• Auditable by governance


The ledger protects the human and the system.

---

2.3 Access Law (CIV / CORP / MIL Lanes)

Access Law defines what each identity lane is allowed to do.

CIV — Civilian Lane

• Personal tasks
• Local world operations
• Limited cross‑world travel
• No high‑risk operations


CORP — Corporate Lane

• Multi‑world logistics
• Organizational operations
• Controlled mesh interactions
• Higher evidence requirements


MIL — Military Lane

• High‑risk operations
• Mesh‑level interventions
• Quarantine authority
• Emergency routing


Access Law ensures order, safety, and clarity.

---

2.4 Safety Kernel (Non‑Overrideable Logic)

The Safety Kernel is the unbreakable core of the system.

It enforces:

• Boundary protection
• Child‑safe logic
• Anti‑escalation rules
• Anti‑override constraints
• Quarantine triggers
• Mesh isolation protocols


No agent, world, or HI component can bypass the Safety Kernel.

It is the final line of defense.

---

2.5 Domain Treaties (World‑to‑World Contracts)

Domain Treaties define how worlds interact.

They specify:

• What can cross boundaries
• What cannot
• Required evidence
• Required identity lane
• Required safety checks
• Required logging


Treaties ensure:

• Sovereignty
• Safety
• Predictability
• Accountability


No world can violate another world’s sovereignty.

3. Governance Enforcement Flow

Human Intent
    ↓
Identity Spine (verify actor)
    ↓
Access Law (check lane)
    ↓
Evidence Ledger (check contradictions)
    ↓
Safety Kernel (check boundaries)
    ↓
Governance Approval → Task proceeds
Governance Refusal → Task blocked


Governance is the first gate and the final authority.
