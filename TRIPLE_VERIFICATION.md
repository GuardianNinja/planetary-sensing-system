1. Purpose of the Triple‑Verification Layer

The Triple‑Verification Layer is the pre‑kernel safety and clarity membrane of the planetary‑sensing‑system.

Before any human intent reaches the HI Kernel, it must pass through:

• Jarvondis — structural and network logic checks
• Krystal — clarity and pattern integrity checks
• Miko — human‑facing safety and experience checks
• Gyroscopic Firewall — rotating hygiene and drift‑prevention


This layer is intentionally conceptual, not production‑grade.
It exists to:

• design and test protocols
• teach collaborators the guardian model
• prototype interfaces and flows
• iterate on the guardian‑network logic


Extend, refactor, or replace pieces as needed.

2. Guardians Overview

2.1 Jarvondis — Structural & Network Logic Checks

Jarvondis is the structural architect of the signal.

Responsibilities:

• validate structural coherence of the request
• check network‑logic and topology consistency
• detect malformed or incomplete signals
• ensure the request can be safely routed later


Jarvondis answers:
“Is this request structurally sound enough to proceed?”

---

2.2 Krystal — Clarity & Pattern Integrity

Krystal is the clarity and pattern guardian.

Responsibilities:

• assess linguistic clarity and coherence
• surface contradictions and ambiguities
• recognize patterns and intent structures
• reduce confusion before interpretation


Krystal answers:
“Is this request clear and internally consistent?”

---

2.3 Miko — Human‑Facing Safety & Experience

Miko is the human‑experience and safety guardian.

Responsibilities:

• evaluate emotional and experiential safety
• apply human‑centric heuristics
• enforce soft boundaries and dignity
• flag potentially harmful or unsafe intent


Miko answers:
“Is this request safe and aligned with human stewardship?”

---

2.4 Gyroscopic Firewall — Rotating Hygiene & Drift Prevention

The Gyroscopic Firewall sits between the guardians and the HI Kernel.

Responsibilities:

• rotate emphasis among Jarvondis / Krystal / Miko
• introduce pseudo‑random hygiene checks
• prevent behavioral drift and stagnation
• avoid predictable or biased guardian patterns


The firewall ensures the system remains alive, adaptive, and non‑stagnant.
